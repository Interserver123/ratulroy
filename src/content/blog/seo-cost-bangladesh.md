SEO in Bangladesh has no fixed price. Cost depends on four things: how competitive your industry and area are, how many locations or service areas you need to rank in, the current state of your website and Google Business Profile, and whether you want strategy only or full implementation. Packages start from $299, quoted after an audit.

SEO pricing in Bangladesh spans a wider range than almost any other service, and providers rarely publish figures. That silence benefits sellers and leaves owners unable to judge whether a quote is fair. This is what actually drives the number. If you are pricing Local SEO specifically (Google Maps and the Map Pack), the companion piece on [how much Local SEO costs in Bangladesh](/blog/local-seo-cost-bangladesh) goes deeper on that.

## What changes the price of SEO?

Four things, in roughly this order of impact: how competitive your area and industry are, how many locations or service areas you need to rank in, the current state of your profile and website, and whether you want strategy only or full implementation.

A single-location business in a quiet district town with a healthy website is a fundamentally different job from a multi-branch operation competing in Gulshan, Dhanmondi and Uttara with a site that needs rebuilding first. A dental clinic or law firm in Dhaka faces more competition than a hardware shop in Bogura.

- Competition in your area and trade
- Number of locations or service areas
- Condition of the existing profile and website
- Strategy only, or done-for-you implementation

## Why very cheap packages fail

At the bottom of the market, the economics do not permit real work. If a package costs less than a few hours of skilled time, it is being delivered by automation or by someone doing the same template for two hundred clients.

The usual outputs are bulk directory submissions on sites nobody visits, spun content, and a monthly PDF of rankings for keywords nobody searches. It looks like activity and produces nothing.

## What a reasonable engagement includes

Google Business Profile optimization done properly, citation and NAP consistency work, on-page and technical fixes on the site, a review strategy, geo-grid tracking so progress is measurable across the whole area, and reporting you can read in two minutes.

## One-off audit vs ongoing work

Not everyone needs a monthly engagement. A standalone audit tells you what is wrong and what order to fix it in, and some businesses take that to an in-house team.

That is often the sensible starting point if you are not sure whether you need ongoing help at all. You can start with a [free visibility audit](/#free-audit).

## SEO cost compared with Google Ads

Google Ads charges per click and stops the day the budget stops. SEO costs more effort up front, but Map Pack and organic visibility keeps producing calls without a per-click charge. Many businesses use ads to fill the gap while SEO builds. I compare the two properly in [Local SEO vs Google Ads](/blog/local-seo-vs-google-ads-bangladesh).

## How to judge a quote

Ask what specifically happens in month one. A fair quote answers with tasks. A weak one answers with adjectives.

Then ask what happens if it does not work, and what you keep if you leave. The answers to those two tell you more about the provider than the price does.
