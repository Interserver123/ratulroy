To choose an SEO specialist in Bangladesh, check four things before price: verifiable results (Search Console or Google Business Profile data with dates), a clear answer about what they will measure, a contract without long lock-ins where you own every account, and someone who explains the work plainly. For a local business, Local SEO experience beats agency size.

Hiring an SEO specialist in Bangladesh is difficult for one reason: the work is invisible until it is finished, and by then you have already paid. Anyone can produce a report. Far fewer can produce customers. These are the checks that separate the two, in the order they are worth doing. If you are not yet sure what the job involves, read [what an SEO specialist actually does](/blog/what-does-seo-specialist-do) first.

## Ask what they will measure first

A specialist should be able to tell you, in the first conversation, which numbers they expect to move and roughly when. Calls, direction requests, form submissions, booked jobs. If the answer is only rankings, be careful. A number one position that produces no calls is a screenshot, not a result.

Ask specifically: what does month three look like, and what does month six look like? A practitioner who has done this before will answer that comfortably, with caveats about your competition. A reseller will change the subject to packages.

## Specialist or generalist?

Bangladesh has plenty of agencies offering SEO alongside paid ads, social media, design and app development. That breadth is fine for some businesses, but it means SEO is one of six things competing for the same team's attention.

A specialist does one thing repeatedly and sees the same patterns across accounts. For a local service business, that pattern recognition matters more than agency size. I compare the two honestly in [SEO specialist vs SEO agency in Bangladesh](/blog/seo-specialist-vs-agency-bangladesh).

- Ask what percentage of their work is SEO versus other services
- Ask which industries they work in most often
- Ask what they have stopped offering, and why

## Look for proof you can verify

Case studies with round numbers and no source are decoration. Ask for screenshots from Google Search Console or Google Business Profile Performance, with the date range visible. Those are harder to massage than a designed report. Public reviews on platforms like Fiverr or Google, from clients you could in principle contact, add a second layer.

You can see what that should look like on my [case studies page](/case-studies). For example, the [GBP calls case study](/case-studies/gbp-calls-growth) shows the Business Profile screenshot behind 461 calls from April to August 2024.

## What are the red flags when hiring an SEO specialist?

- Guaranteed first position or "page one in 30 days"
- Bulk packages of hundreds of backlinks or directory submissions
- Offers to write or buy reviews for you
- Keyword-stuffed business names or fake addresses on your Google Business Profile
- Refusing to give you admin access to your own accounts
- Reports full of rankings for keywords nobody searches

Any one of these is enough to walk away. They produce a short-term chart and a long-term problem, sometimes a suspended profile.

## Understand the contract first

Long lock-in contracts exist to protect the provider, not you. A specialist confident in the work earns the next month by delivering this one.

Ask what happens if you want to stop after ninety days. Ask who owns the Google Business Profile, the website, the Google Analytics 4 property and the Search Console account when the relationship ends. The answer should be: you do, always.

## Test how they explain things

You are hiring someone whose work you cannot directly inspect, so the ability to explain it plainly is not a soft skill here; it is the main safeguard you have.

If a first conversation leaves you more confused than when you started, that will not improve once money changes hands.

## Realistic price and timeline

Local SEO is not instant. Three to six months to measurable movement is normal, with compounding gains after that, and the starting condition of your profile and website changes everything. I break that down in [how long Local SEO takes in Bangladesh](/blog/how-long-local-seo-takes-bangladesh).

On price, published entry figures in this market start around a few hundred dollars a month and rise with competition and the number of areas you need to cover. Anyone quoting a firm number before seeing your profile is quoting a package, not a plan. The full breakdown is in [how much SEO costs in Bangladesh](/blog/seo-cost-bangladesh).

The best signal is simple: does this person tell you things you did not want to hear? Someone willing to say a keyword is unrealistic, or that your site needs work before rankings will move, is someone worth hiring.
