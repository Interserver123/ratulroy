Local SEO ranks a business, mainly through its Google Business Profile, in the map pack and on Google Maps for searches tied to a place, such as 'dentist in Uttara' or 'AC repair near me'. Traditional SEO ranks web pages in the ordinary results for searches with no location attached. Most local businesses in Bangladesh need Local SEO first.

Business owners often hear 'SEO' and 'Local SEO' used as if they were the same service. They overlap, but they rank different things, in different places, for different kinds of search. Choosing the wrong one wastes months. This guide, part 5 of the [Local SEO in Bangladesh series](/blog/local-seo-bangladesh-guide), sets out the differences plainly and helps you decide which your business needs first.

## The core difference

Traditional SEO, sometimes called organic or national SEO, is about ranking web pages in the ordinary list of search results for searches with no particular location attached: 'how to choose a laptop', 'best accounting software', 'IELTS preparation tips'. Local SEO is about ranking a business, mainly through its Google Business Profile, for searches where the location matters: 'laptop repair Elephant Road', 'accountant in Motijheel', 'IELTS coaching near me'.

> Put simply, traditional SEO competes to be the best page on a subject. Local SEO competes to be the best business in a place.

## Where the results appear

- **Local SEO:** the map pack of three businesses, the full local finder list, and Google Maps itself
- **Traditional SEO:** the ordinary blue-link results, and increasingly the sources cited in AI Overviews
- **Both:** ordinary results for local searches, where your website competes against other local sites and directories

For a search like 'dentist in Uttara', the map pack usually appears above the ordinary results, which is why local businesses get most of their calls from Local SEO rather than from their website's organic position.

## What each one ranks

Traditional SEO ranks pages. Each page competes on its own merits for the searches it targets, and a strong site can have hundreds of pages ranking for different topics.

Local SEO ranks listings. Your Google Business Profile is one entity, tied to one location, competing for every local search in your category. The website supports it but is not what appears in the map pack.

## Ranking signals compared

| Signal | Local SEO | Traditional SEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Central (distance from the searcher) | Largely irrelevant |
| Google Business Profile | The main asset | Not a factor |
| Reviews | A major signal | Minor |
| Citations and NAP consistency | Important | Barely relevant |
| Content depth | Important | The main factor |
| Backlinks | Important, with local relevance counting for more | Important |
| Technical health | Important; a broken site hurts | Important; a broken site hurts |
| Structured data | LocalBusiness schema and subtypes such as Dentist | Article, Product and FAQPage schema |
| Main measurement | Calls and direction requests in GBP Performance | Clicks and impressions in Google Search Console |

## Keywords: with and without a place

Local keywords carry a place, either stated ('in Chattogram', 'Dhanmondi') or implied ('near me', or simply 'pharmacy' typed on a phone, which Google often treats as local). Traditional keywords do not. Volumes differ enormously as a result. A local keyword might be searched a few dozen times a month, but almost every one of those searches is a potential customer nearby. A national keyword may be searched thousands of times by people who will never buy from you.

This is also why keyword research tools often undersell local opportunity. The low numbers they show for area-specific searches hide very high purchase intent.

## How success is measured

- **Local SEO:** calls, direction requests, website clicks from the profile, and visibility across a geo-grid of your area
- **Traditional SEO:** organic traffic, rankings for target pages, and conversions from that traffic

Mixing these up leads to bad decisions. A local business judged only on website traffic may look like it is failing while the profile quietly produces most of its calls.

## Timelines and cost

Local SEO tends to show movement sooner, because the competition is limited to an area and profile fixes take effect relatively quickly. Traditional SEO is usually slower, because you compete nationally or globally and content needs time to earn links and trust. The [data-driven organic growth case study](/case-studies/data-driven-organic-growth) shows a realistic pace: a near-invisible site taken from almost zero to 3.93K clicks and 47.4K impressions over six months.

## Which one does your business need?

- **Clinics, restaurants, salons, repair services, showrooms and coaching centres:** Local SEO first
- **Home service businesses that visit customers:** Local SEO first, set up as a service-area business
- **E-commerce shops delivering nationwide across Dhaka, Chattogram and beyond:** traditional SEO first
- **Software, IT and export businesses selling abroad:** traditional SEO first
- **Businesses with branches in several cities:** Local SEO for each branch, plus traditional SEO for the brand
- **Professional firms such as lawyers and accountants:** both, with Local SEO usually paying back sooner

## Why most need both

Part 4, [Why Local SEO Is Important](/blog/why-local-seo-important-bangladesh), covers the business case for the local side. The two disciplines support each other. Google has said that a business's position in ordinary web results contributes to its local prominence, so a stronger website helps the profile. And content written for traditional SEO, answering customers' questions, brings in people earlier in their decision who later search locally.

The right order for most local businesses is Local SEO first, because it produces calls soonest, followed by website content and technical work that strengthen both. The [Local SEO service](/local-seo-service) covers the local side, and the [technical SEO service](/technical-seo) covers the website foundations: crawling, indexing and Core Web Vitals.

## A quick decision checklist

- Do customers come to you, or you to them, within a particular area? **Local SEO.**
- Do you sell to anyone in the country or the world, with no local element? **Traditional SEO.**
- Do people search for your service with a place name or 'near me'? **Local SEO.**
- Do people research your topic before buying, without a place in mind? **Traditional SEO content.**
- Answered yes to both groups? **You need both, local first.**

> Local SEO and traditional SEO are related disciplines aimed at different targets: one ranks a business in a place, the other ranks pages on a subject. For most businesses serving customers in Bangladesh, Local SEO comes first because it reaches the people ready to buy nearby.
